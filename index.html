For university students in Hong Kong

Stress doesn't always shout. Sometimes it just shows up quietly.

Most university students feel stressed at some point — you're not alone. But when stress sticks around, it leaves small signals in how you sleep, focus, and show up around others. This site helps you notice them — and reminds you that reaching out is normal, not a failure.

Why "small signals"?

Stress among university students is common — but it's also easy to miss, because the early signs look like ordinary student life: trouble sleeping, difficulty concentrating, skipping meals, or pulling back from friends. We often tell ourselves it's "just deadlines" or "I'll manage."

The problem isn't feeling stressed. The problem is when those signals pile up and we don't notice — or don't feel we can ask for help. In a 2025 Hong Kong survey, 55% of adults said they would not, or were unsure about, seeking professional help. This site is a small invitation to notice the signals early and take one gentle step.
