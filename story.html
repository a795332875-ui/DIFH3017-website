A day with Jay

Jay is a fictional second-year university student in Hong Kong. Nothing here describes a real person. As you go through his day, you'll be asked what you'd do — but there are no right or wrong answers, no scores, and no labels. Each choice opens a short note about what the signal might mean.

Content note: This story touches on stress, sleeplessness, and feeling overwhelmed. You can stop at any time and jump to support information.

The page's light follows Jay's day — from 3 a.m. dark to evening lamp.
