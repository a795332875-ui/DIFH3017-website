You don't have to carry it alone

This page lists verified support resources for Hong Kong and international readers. You do not need a diagnosis to reach out — and reaching out is not the same as being "sick." Sometimes the first step is just talking to someone.

If you or someone near you is in immediate danger, call your local emergency number right away: 999 in Hong Kong, or the emergency services in your country. If you're unsure, go to the nearest hospital emergency department.

If you want to talk to a friend or family member

You don't need to have all the answers. The first message doesn't have to be perfect. Here are some gentle starting points:

"I've noticed you seem a bit off lately. Want to talk about it? No pressure."

"How are you really doing? I'm here, even if you don't have words yet."

"I've been finding things hard too. Want to grab a coffee and not talk about anything heavy?"

If someone opens up to you, the most helpful thing you can do is listen without fixing. You can say: "That sounds really heavy. I'm glad you told me."

Small steps count. One conversation, one walk, one message. You don't have to solve everything at once.

Everyday things that can help

These are not cures, and they don't replace professional support. But they are evidence-informed ways to look after yourself when things feel heavy:

🌙 Sleep

Even small, consistent sleep improvements can ease stress. A regular bedtime and a screen-free wind-down help.

🚶 Movement

A 15-minute walk outdoors can shift mood. You don't need a full workout — movement is movement.

🍎 Routine

Eating regularly and keeping some structure to your day stabilises mood and energy.

👥 Connection

Even brief, low-effort contact with someone you trust can lighten the load. Isolation makes everything harder.
